Privacy notice.

Effective 3 November 2026. This notice explains how Tinlance Limited handles information submitted through the Tinlyt public website and early-access request form. The authenticated workspace and investigation services are not yet generally available and are not covered as active processing services by this launch notice.

1. Who is responsible

Tinlance Limited is the data controller for the processing described here. Address: 120 Okigwe Road, Owerri, Imo State, Nigeria. Privacy enquiries and rights requests: sales@tinlance.com (use the subject “Tinlyt privacy request”).

2. Information we collect

When you submit an early-access request, we collect the information you choose to provide: name, email address, company or project, role, selected use case, optional workflow description, and the date and version of the notice you accepted. We separately record whether you opted in to product updates and the time of that choice. Marketing consent is optional and is not required to submit a request.

To protect the form, Cloudflare Turnstile may process browser, device and interaction signals. Our rate limiter stores keyed HMAC hashes derived from request identifiers such as an IP address or email address; it does not store the raw IP address in the rate-limit table. Do not submit passwords, API keys, private keys, confidential customer information or sensitive personal data.

3. Why we use the information

  • Review and respond to your request: to assess the use case you submitted and contact you about the request. We rely on your affirmative submission and, where applicable, steps taken at your request before entering a potential service agreement.
  • Service security and abuse prevention: to validate the request, limit automated abuse and protect the website. We rely on our legitimate interests in operating a secure request channel, balanced against your rights.
  • Optional product updates: to send occasional product news only if you separately opt in. You can withdraw that consent at any time.
  • Legal and compliance matters: where processing is necessary to meet a legal obligation or establish, exercise or defend a legal claim.

4. Service providers

We use the following providers for the early-access workflow and public website:

  • Vercel hosts the public website and runs its serverless request endpoint. Vercel states that its services operate across AWS, Microsoft Azure and Google Cloud infrastructure and that backups may be globally replicated. Processing locations can therefore vary by service and provider configuration.
  • Supabase stores early-access requests and rate-limit records. The dedicated Tinlyt project is hosted in AWS region eu-west-1 (Ireland). Supabase states that core project data is primarily stored in the selected region, while limited support and subprocessor processing may occur elsewhere.
  • Resend sends the applicant confirmation and internal request notification. The sending domain is configured for the eu-west-1 sending region; Resend states that customer data, including message content and delivery logs, is stored in the United States.
  • Cloudflare provides Turnstile anti-abuse verification. Cloudflare processes challenge signals to provide the service and may separately process signals to improve its detection capabilities, as described in its Turnstile privacy addendum.

These providers process information under their applicable service terms and data-protection commitments. Their current subprocessor lists and terms may change; links are available from their privacy and legal documentation.

5. International processing and transfers

Your request information may be processed in Nigeria, the European Union (including Ireland) and the United States, and may be accessed in other locations by provider personnel or subprocessors as described in the providers’ documentation. In particular, Supabase hosts the request database in Ireland, while Resend stores email data in the United States. Vercel and Cloudflare operate global services.

We limit transfers to what is needed to host the website, store and review your request, prevent abuse and send the communications you requested. Tinlance will document the applicable transfer basis and safeguards for each provider and destination under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, including sections 41–42 and the NDPC’s GAID 2025. Provider contractual terms, including standard contractual clauses where applicable, are considered as part of that assessment; an EU transfer clause is not represented as an automatic Nigerian adequacy decision. If a required Nigerian transfer safeguard is not in place, we will not open the request form for new submissions.

6. How long we keep information

  • Early-access requests: up to 12 months from submission, then deleted or irreversibly anonymised unless a longer period is required for a legal claim or legal obligation.
  • Rate-limit hashes: no longer than 24 hours after the last update, with hourly cleanup scheduled so deletion occurs within approximately 25 hours.
  • Marketing preference: retained with the request record and deleted with it, or earlier when you withdraw consent.
  • Provider email and operational logs: retained according to the relevant provider’s current plan, security and deletion policies. Provider backups may persist for their documented backup-expiry period.

You may ask us to delete your request sooner. A deletion request does not require you to explain why. We may retain a minimal record where necessary to demonstrate that a request was handled or to meet a legal obligation.

7. Your data subject rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, deletion of, restriction of or portability of your personal data; object to processing based on legitimate interests; and withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out lawfully. To exercise a right, email sales@tinlance.com with “Tinlyt privacy request” in the subject. We may ask for reasonable information to verify that you are the person concerned. You may also lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

8. Security

We apply access restrictions to the intake database, keep browser roles from reading or writing request records, use server-side anti-abuse checks and protect rate-limit identifiers with keyed hashes. Information is transmitted to providers over encrypted connections. No internet service can guarantee absolute security; we maintain procedures to investigate and respond to suspected incidents in accordance with applicable law.

9. Cookies and similar technologies

The current public landing pages do not intentionally deploy advertising or analytics cookies. The early-access form uses Cloudflare Turnstile when the feature is activated; that service may process technical signals for abuse prevention. Provider infrastructure may use strictly necessary technologies to deliver and secure the service. If we introduce analytics, advertising or other non-essential tracking, we will update this notice and provide the required choices before enabling it.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this notice when the service, providers or legal requirements change. The version and effective date will be updated on this page, and the version accepted with an early-access request is recorded. For questions or complaints, contact Tinlance Limited at the address and email in section 1.

This notice is limited to the public website and early-access request workflow. Before enabling additional Tinlyt workspace, account, investigation, browser or evidence-processing features, Tinlance will update the notice to describe those data flows and complete the applicable privacy and cross-border transfer assessments.
