Start with the requirements.

For evaluation, integration and enterprise discussions, prepare a short description of the research workflow, required data boundaries and the decision the evidence should support.

Product evaluation

Describe your use case, expected source types, review process and how fresh the information needs to be.

Integration discussion

Identify your consumer environment, identity requirements, API/MCP needs and the exact systems that must exchange evidence.

Security review

List required assurance evidence, data-retention constraints, egress policy and operational recovery expectations.
